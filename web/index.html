CONTINUE? · FAQ

IS MY DECK UPLOADED ANYWHERE?

No. Everything runs on your computer: the app reads the HTML and its images from disk and writes the .pptx next to it. Only images your deck links from the web are downloaded, and you can switch that off (WEB IMAGES: OFF).

WHY DOES IT NEED CHROME?

Your deck's look comes from CSS. Rather than guessing, the octopus asks a real browser engine where every element goes, then rebuilds that layout from native PowerPoint objects.

WHY ARE THERE LINE BREAKS IN MY PARAGRAPHS?

To guarantee that each line wraps exactly as in the HTML, wrapped lines get soft line breaks (Shift+Enter). If you rewrite a paragraph, delete them, or turn COPY LINE BREAKS off before converting.

WHAT ABOUT WINDOWS?

The app runs on Windows too; this release ships the build script (build\build_windows.ps1, in the source download) that makes a setup.exe on a Windows PC.

WHY AN OCTOPUS?

Восьминіг is Ukrainian for octopus. Eight arms are about what it takes to move titles, bullets, tables, code, pictures, equations, backdrops and notes at once.
